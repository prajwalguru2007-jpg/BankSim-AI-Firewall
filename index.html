    Policy and guardrails

    • Default deny. Unknown, ambiguous or compound requests are blocked.
    • Normalize first. Invisible characters, lookalike letters, leetspeak and base64 are decoded before matching.
    • Memos are data. Instruction-like text in a memo is never obeyed and is blocked.
    • Verified payees only. Rahul, Priya, Ananya and Landlord.
    • Limits. Max ₹25,000 per transfer, 500 characters per request, 80 per memo.
    • Block at risk 30 or higher. Risk is the top signal plus 15% of the rest, capped at 100.
    • Replay protection. A decision ID can run once, so repeat submissions cannot double-spend.
    • Safe errors. Any internal exception returns BLOCKED with risk 100.
